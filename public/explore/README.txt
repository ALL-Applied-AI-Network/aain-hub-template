The two photographs here are of ALL Applied AI Network chapter events and are
used as the default illustration on the Explore blocks, for chapters that have
not written their own. Each is captioned on the page as being from "a network
chapter" rather than from the chapter whose site is showing it — an uncaptioned
photo on a club's own page claims to be that club.

  project-kickoff.jpg  a speaker night, an industry sponsor presenting
  build-night.jpg      two students demoing their project

A chapter's own image always wins over these; an authored image is never
captioned. Replace or remove them only together with the copy in renderExplore
(src/main.ts) that names them.
